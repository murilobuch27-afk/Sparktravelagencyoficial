The destination

An archipelago
built for slow travel

Off the coast of West Africa, Cabo Verde blends Portuguese colonial architecture, Creole culture and some of the calmest turquoise water in the Atlantic. Ten islands, each with a completely different personality — from the desert dunes of Sal to the green peaks of Santo Antão.

This package is built around Sal and Santiago, the two islands that pair the best beaches with the richest history, connected by short domestic flights arranged entirely by our team.

Trip itinerary

Six days across two islands

A pace built for actually resting — mornings unhurried, afternoons exploring, evenings on the water.

DAY 01GRU → SID

Arrival in Sal

Landing at Amílcar Cabral International Airport, transfer to your beachfront hotel in Santa Maria, welcome dinner facing the Atlantic.

DAY 02SANTA MARIA

Santa Maria Beach

A full day on one of the best beaches in West Africa — white sand, calm water, and time for windsurfing or simply doing nothing.

DAY 03SAL ISLAND

Pedra de Lume & Buracona

Float in a volcanic crater salt lake, then watch the sunlight pour through the "Blue Eye" at Buracona's natural pool.

DAY 04COASTLINE

Lighthouse Coastal Walk

A guided walk along the cliffs to the red-and-white Farol de Santa Maria lighthouse, followed by a sunset catamaran cruise.

DAY 05SID → RAI

Cidade Velha, Santiago

Short flight to Praia and a guided tour through Cabo Verde's first colonial town, cobblestones and the Fort Real de São Filipe.

DAY 06RAI → GRU

Farewell

A free morning for last-minute souvenirs in the local market, then transfer to the airport for your TAP Air Portugal flight home.
